Features: rknative: Neon, ArmAes, ArmSha1, ArmSha2, ArmSha512, ArmSha3
          .NET: AdvSimd, Aes, Sha256 supported; Vector128 accelerated, Vector256 NOT accelerated

Implementation notes (single-thread microbenchmarks on a P-core)
  - RAR 3: the AVX2 16-lane SHA-1 kernel compiled for NEON (clang vector types, two q registers
    per 8 lanes) runs ~650 candidates/s; a version on the SHA-1 instructions (sha1c/p/m,
    sha1su0/1, 4 streams interleaved) reached only ~460/s because sha1c issues once per ~4 cycles
    on the M1, so it was dropped.
  - PDF R6: AESE/AESMC (8 CBC chains interleaved), SHA256H/H2 (4 streams), SHA512H/H2 (2 streams;
    1 stream -11 %, 4 streams -2 %, 8 streams -14 %), ~7,800 candidates/s at length 7.
  - RC4 filters: the RC4 key schedule (8 interleaved streams, C) runs at ~1.66 cycles per byte and
    dominates PDF R3 (20 schedules per candidate); the x86 build keeps its hand-written assembly.
  - ZipCrypto: AdvSimd path with 4 x 4 lanes and CRC lookups through tbl (two 16-entry nibble
    tables) is 1.6x the scalar path; it is the default on arm64 (RECOVERKIT_ZIP_NEON=0 disables).
  - The managed Vector256 kernels stay disabled on arm64: 2.5-9x slower than the native paths.
